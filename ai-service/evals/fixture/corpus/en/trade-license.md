# Trade License Application

## Overview

A trade license authorizes a business to carry out its commercial activity. It is issued by the trade licensing authority of the city or region where the business operates. Operating without a valid trade license is an offense. The license must be renewed periodically, usually once a year.

## Applying for a trade license

A business applies for a trade license with its application for business name registration or separately after registration. The application states the business activity, address, and legal form. The license lists the specific activity the business may carry out; a business that changes activity must apply for a new license or an amendment.

## Fees and duration

The trade license fee depends on the business activity and the city or region. The license is valid for one year from the date of issue. Renewal is required before the expiry date. Late renewal attracts a penalty and continued operation after expiry is treated as unlicensed operation.

## Conditions attached to the license

Certain activities require additional permits before a trade license is issued. Examples include food handling, pharmaceuticals, and activities with public health or safety implications. The business must present these permits with its license application.

## Display of the license

The trade license must be displayed in a visible place at the business premises. Authorities may request the license during inspections. The license number is also required for several business transactions, including bank account operations and tax filings.
