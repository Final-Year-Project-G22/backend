# MSME Formalization: Step-by-Step Overview

## Overview

Formalization is the process of moving a business from informal operation to fully registered and licensed status. The process follows a standard sequence: name selection, business registration, TIN acquisition, trade licensing, and ongoing compliance.

## Step 1: Business name selection

The business selects a name and checks its availability with the registration authority. The name must not duplicate an existing registered name and must comply with naming rules. For a share company, the name must include the legal form indicator.

## Step 2: Business registration

The business registers its legal form with the registration authority. A share company submits notarized memorandum and articles of association. A sole proprietor registers the business name and activity. Registration produces the registration certificate and registration number.

## Step 3: Tax registration

After registration, the business obtains a TIN from the tax authority. If annual turnover reaches the VAT threshold, the business must also register for VAT. The TIN is used for all subsequent tax filings and is required for opening bank accounts.

## Step 4: Trade licensing

The business applies for a trade license for its specific activity at the trade licensing authority of its city or region. Additional permits may be required for regulated activities. The license must be renewed annually.

## Step 5: Ongoing compliance

Once formalized, the business must file tax returns on time, renew its trade license before expiry, update registrations when details change, and keep proper records.
