# VAT Registration for MSMEs in Ethiopia

## Overview

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax applied to the supply of goods and services in Ethiopia. Businesses whose annual taxable turnover meets the registration threshold must register for VAT with the tax authority. VAT registration is mandatory, not optional, once the threshold is met.

## Registration threshold

A business must register for VAT when its annual taxable turnover reaches or exceeds the registration threshold. The threshold applies to the taxable supply of goods and services. Once the threshold is crossed, the business must apply for VAT registration within thirty days of the end of the month in which the threshold was reached.

## Obligations of a VAT-registered business

A VAT-registered business must issue a valid tax invoice for every taxable supply. The tax invoice must show the VAT amount separately. Registered businesses must also file VAT returns on a regular basis and pay the tax due on time. Failure to file or pay on time attracts penalties.

## Exempt supplies

Certain supplies are exempt from VAT. A business that makes only exempt supplies is not required to register for VAT, even if its turnover exceeds the threshold. Businesses that make both taxable and exempt supplies must register for VAT and may only recover input VAT on the taxable portion.

## Consequences of late registration

A business that fails to register for VAT after crossing the threshold faces penalties. The tax authority may also collect the VAT that should have been charged from the date the registration obligation arose.
