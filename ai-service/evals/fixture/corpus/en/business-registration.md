# Business Registration in Ethiopia

## Overview

Business registration is the process of formally registering a business with the responsible government authority. Registration gives a business legal personality, enables it to open bank accounts, and is a precondition for many licenses and permits. Registration requirements differ by legal form.

## Registration for share companies

A share company (PLC) must register with the ministry responsible for trade. The registration is based on the Commercial Code. A PLC must have its memorandum and articles of association notarized, then submit them with the registration application. Registration certificates are issued with a unique registration number.

## Registration for sole proprietors

A sole proprietor registers their business name and activity with the same authority that handles trade licensing. Sole proprietors register under their own name or a trade name. The registration is simpler and faster than for a share company.

## Required documents

Common requirements include: notarized memorandum and articles (for share companies), identification of owners and directors, proof of address of the business premises, and the taxpayer identification number of the owners. Documents in a foreign language must be accompanied by a translation.

## Renewal and updates

Business registration is not renewed annually; it is a one-time registration. However, changes to the business name, legal form, ownership, or registered address must be registered with an update filing. Operating under a changed name without registering the update is a compliance violation.
