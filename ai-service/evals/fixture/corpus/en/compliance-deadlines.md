# Compliance Deadlines and Obligations

## Overview

Formalized businesses in Ethiopia face a set of recurring compliance deadlines. Missing a deadline can result in penalties, interest, or loss of the underlying license or registration. This document summarizes the main obligations every MSME should track.

## Tax filings

A VAT-registered business must file its VAT return and pay the tax due on the filing deadline of each reporting period. Businesses must also file income tax declarations and pay the tax due before the annual deadline. Late filing and late payment both attract penalties and interest.

## License renewals

Trade licenses must be renewed before their expiry date each year. Renewal requires payment of the renewal fee and, in some cases, updated permits. A business that lets its license lapse must stop operating until the license is renewed.

## Registration updates

Changes to business name, address, legal form, or ownership must be filed with the registration authority within the time allowed after the change. The taxpayer identification number record must be updated with the tax authority for changes of address or activity.

## Record keeping

A business must keep books and records sufficient to support its tax declarations. Records must be kept for the period required by law. Missing records can make it impossible to defend a filing during an audit, and can lead to estimated assessments.
