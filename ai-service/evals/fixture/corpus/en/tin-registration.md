# TIN Registration for Businesses

## Overview

The Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is the unique registration number assigned to every taxpayer in Ethiopia. Every business must obtain a TIN before it can register for VAT, open a bank account, or complete other registration steps. The TIN is issued once and is used for all tax-related transactions.

## Who needs a TIN

Every business, regardless of size or legal form, must have a TIN. Sole proprietorships, partnerships, and share companies all register for a TIN. Even a business that does not yet owe tax must register if it is engaged in any taxable activity.

## How to register for a TIN

A business registers for a TIN at the tax authority office that serves its area of operation. The applicant must present identification for the business owner and documents showing the business's existence. Registration is free of charge. The TIN is issued immediately in most cases.

## TIN and other registrations

The TIN is a prerequisite for several other steps in formalizing a business. A business needs its TIN to register for VAT, to obtain a trade license in some cases, and to open a business bank account. Banks and government offices commonly ask for the TIN as proof of tax registration.

## Updating TIN information

A business must notify the tax authority of any change in its name, address, business activity, or legal form. Changes are recorded against the same TIN; a new TIN is not issued when a business simply changes its address or activity.
